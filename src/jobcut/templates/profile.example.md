# My profile

> Copy this file to `profile.md` and fill it in honestly, in your own words. This is the
> single highest-leverage input: the scorer compares every job against what's here, and
> your skills "kit" (which drives scoring **and** Discovery) is derived from it. Works for
> any field — designer, product manager, nurse, teacher, analyst… nothing here is tied to a
> role. Garbage profile in, garbage matches out.
>
> Easiest way to fill it: run onboarding, or the **jobcut-profile** skill (it interviews you
> or reads your CV and writes this file + the kit for you). `profile.md` is git-ignored —
> your real profile never gets committed.

## Location & work mode

What counts as a workable job for you. Drives both the geo router and scoring.

- Based in: <your city/country>
- Remote: yes / hybrid only / on-site only
- Willing to relocate: no / for the right role
- Time zone constraints: <e.g. must overlap CET>

## Dealbreakers (hard requirements you do NOT meet)

Jobs that require any of these get penalized.

- Languages you don't speak (e.g. "fluent German required")
- Credentials/licenses you don't hold (e.g. a clearance, a specific certification)
- On-site in a location you can't take
- Domains you won't work in (e.g. gambling, defense)

## Notes for the scorer

Free text. Anything else that should nudge a score up or down — e.g. "prefer
mission-driven orgs", "avoid pure-agency roles", "portfolio-heavy roles are a plus".
