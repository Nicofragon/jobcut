Description

We are looking for an experienced Senior Product Designer to join our core team. You will lead the design of new features and iterate on existing flows to ensure a seamless, delightful experience for our users.

What you'll do:

  • Collaborate tightly with engineering and product management to define solutions.
  • Create high-fidelity mockups, prototypes, and design specs.
  • Conduct user research to validate design decisions.
  • Help maintain and evolve our expanding design system.

Requirements:

  • 5+ years of experience in product design, ideally in SaaS or B2B.
  • Strong portfolio demonstrating systems thinking and visual execution.
  • Proficiency in Figma and modern prototyping tools.
